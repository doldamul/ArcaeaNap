Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how ArcaeaNap handles data. ArcaeaNap is a desktop application designed for Arcaea players.

2. Data Collection and Usage

ArcaeaNap is a local-first application. All sensitive information, including your Arcaea Online credentials and API tokens, are stored locally on your machine. We do not operate a centralized server that collects your personal data.

Google User Data: When you connect your Google account to ArcaeaNap, the application requests access to your email address (for account identification, using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email and openid scopes) and permissions to read and manage specific files in your Google Drive (specifically, the Google Sheet used to store your data, using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope). This data is used solely for the purpose of exporting and synchronizing your local Arcaea play records to your personal Google Sheet. Additionally, the application accesses a public, community-maintained Google Sheet in read-only mode (using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.readonly scope) to fetch the general song data required to generate the local song database.

3. Data Transmission and Sharing

Your data is never shared with or sold to third parties. It is only transmitted directly from your local machine to the following official endpoints as necessary for the application's core functionality:

  • Official Arcaea Online servers (for fetching your play records).
  • Google API endpoints (for syncing your data directly to your personal Google Sheets and fetching public song data).

4. Data Deletion and Retention

Users can revoke the app's access permissions at any time from their Google Account settings. Locally stored information can be completely removed by the user by directly deleting the data folder.

5. Google API Services Limited Use Disclosure

ArcaeaNap's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Security

Since the application runs entirely on your local machine, the security of your data depends on your local system's security. ArcaeaNap uses standard OS-level encryption for sensitive tokens where available.

7. Third-Party Services

ArcaeaNap integrates with lowiro's Arcaea Online and Google Sheets. Please refer to their respective privacy policies regarding how they handle data once it reaches their servers.

8. Contact

For inquiries regarding this policy, please contact arcaeanap@googlegroups.com or reach out via GitHub Issues.
